🎮 Multiplayer Chatroom Games

Players in the same chatroom can compete against each other.

Every player pays the game's Credits entry fee. The combined entry fees become the game pot. The winner receives the pot.

Example:
2 players × 50 Credits = 100 Credits pot.
Winner receives 100 Credits.
