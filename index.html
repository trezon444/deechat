💬 What is DeeChat?
DeeChat is a mobile-friendly community where registered members can connect with other members, participate in discussions and join groups.
💬 Chat
Talk with other DeeChat members.
📣 Shouts
Share quick messages with the community.
📝 Topics
Start discussions and reply to other members.
🏢 Groups
Join communities and organizations created by authorized DeeChat staff.
🏆 Points & Rewards
Participate and earn member points and badges.
🏢 DeeChat Groups
Groups can have their own leaders, co-leaders, ranks, tags and activities.

Example:
🛡️ DPF — DeeChat Police Force
🎵 Music Club
🎮 Gaming Club

You must be logged in to access groups.
🔒 Members Only
DeeChat's community areas are available only to registered and logged-in members.

Visitors cannot access:
❌ Member homepage
❌ Chat rooms
❌ Member list
❌ Profiles
❌ Shouts
❌ Topics
❌ Groups
❌ Private messages
❌ Admin panels
